About

Funding shouldn't be the reason you don't apply.

Every year, strong students hold back from graduate school in the US and Canada because the funding picture looks confusing or out of reach. Abimbola Akinduro works directly with applicants to change that.

The work is practical: identifying the professors and programs that fund students in your field, building applications that speak the language of admissions and funding committees, and following through until an offer with funding is on the table.

Reach out on WhatsApp or by email to start with a conversation about your field, your target schools, and what funding is realistically available to you.

Services

Where the guidance is focused

Assistantship placement

Finding graduate and research assistantship openings that come with tuition waivers and a stipend, and positioning your application to match them.

Scholarships & fellowships

Shortlisting the scholarships and fellowships genuinely open to international applicants in your field, with realistic timelines.

Application & SOP support

Sharpening statements of purpose, CVs and professor outreach emails so funding decision-makers take a second look.
